Questions people actually ask.

Do I have to show my face?

Not once. The avatar is the face, and it stays consistent across your videos so the account can build recognition.

Do I really need 1,000 followers?

To join TikTok Shop Affiliate, yes. That is the platform's requirement, and the guide explains the workflow without pretending the gate does not exist.

Will I get banned for using AI?

Using AI itself is not the issue. Label the content, disclose that the character is AI, and follow the platform's current rules.

I tried AI videos before and nothing happened. Why is this different?

An avatar alone earns nothing. It still needs a suitable product, a sales-focused script and a posting routine. The guide shows how those pieces fit together without promising results.
