Questions before you choose.

Do I have to show my face?

No. The avatar can be the visible identity, but you still direct the character and disclose that it is AI.

Which of the six routes should I start with?

Start with the buyer you can reach and the deliverable you can repeat. The guide separates all six by those two decisions.

Do the tools have extra costs?

Yes. Tool plans vary, and the guide lists the practical setup and costs before you commit to a workflow.

Do I need to disclose that the content uses AI?

Yes. Follow each platform's current labeling rules and never present synthetic expertise or experience as real.

Which languages are included?

English and Spanish. Both PDF editions are included in the same purchase.
